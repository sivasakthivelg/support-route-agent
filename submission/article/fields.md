SupportRoute: Routing Customer Support Messages to the Right Team with Strands Decider 2B and Amazon Bedrock

Description (max 512 characters):
SupportRoute is an AI agent that reads a customer's free-text support message and uses Strands Decider 2B to classify it into billing, technical, account-access, general, or escalation -- then creates a ticket and tells the customer which team will handle them. The decider runs the classification in 3.3 s, 5x faster than asking the LLM the same question.
